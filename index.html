Delete Account — Play Social Games

This page allows you to permanently delete your Play Social Games account and the personal data associated with it.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You must authenticate with Google before deletion can be requested.

Secure account verification

For security reasons, this page does not accept an email address as proof of account ownership. You must sign in with the Google account associated with your Play Social Games account.

The deletion operation is performed only for the authenticated Supabase account. This page never accepts a user ID or account email to choose which account to delete.

Authenticated account

You are authenticated as this account. If this is not the account you intended to delete, sign out and choose the correct Google account.

Confirm permanent deletion

This will permanently delete your Play Social Games account and associated application data.

What will be deleted

  • Your Play Social Games account.
  • Your profile and associated account data.
  • Your game-related data associated with the account.
  • Other data that the application deletes as part of its account-deletion procedure.

The public Supabase publishable key used by this page is not a secret. Never place a Supabase service-role or secret key in this file.
