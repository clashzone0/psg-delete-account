Delete Account — Play Social Games

This page allows you to request deletion of your Play Social Games account and the personal data associated with it.

Important: Submitting a request does not automatically delete an account. To protect accounts from unauthorized deletion requests, account ownership must be verified through a secure authentication process before any deletion is performed.

How to request account deletion

  1. Sign in using the Google account associated with your Play Social Games account.
  2. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.
  3. The deletion request is processed only for the authenticated account.

For security reasons, this page must never delete an account based only on an email address, a user ID, or information entered manually by another person.

What will be deleted

Once the request has been authenticated and processed, the account and associated personal data will be deleted in accordance with the Play Social Games Privacy Policy, except for information that must be retained when required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

See also the Privacy Policy.
